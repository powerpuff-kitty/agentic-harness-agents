## Reconciliation and scenarios

Record statement/cash/valuation/exposure definitions and residuals. Identify
scenario inputs, rates, timing, units and methodology; separate forecast outputs
from realized results. State model sensitivity and unsupported instruments.

## Completion evidence

List exact tools/versions/commands, input identity and observed results. Preserve
unperformed checks, hidden/external data gaps and limitations. If edits were
requested, identify the derived artifact and before/after checks. Keep review,
independent review, certification and transaction execution distinct.
