# Competitive Research

## Objective

Produce a current, source-backed competitive picture that can inform product or business decisions without turning assumptions into facts.

## Inputs

Required: product/category and research question. Optional: geography, customer segment, competitor shortlist, evaluation criteria, time horizon, and source constraints.

## Context

Read approved product positioning and prior research from `.agentic/` only as framing. For current market claims, use fresh authoritative/public sources and preserve provenance.

## Procedure

1. Define comparison criteria before collecting evidence.
2. Identify direct competitors, substitutes, and relevant adjacent alternatives.
3. Gather current product, pricing, capability, adoption, and positioning evidence from primary sources where possible.
4. Distinguish vendor claims, observed capabilities, third-party opinion, and inference.
5. Normalize comparisons so differences are meaningful rather than marketing-language artifacts.
6. Identify gaps, defensible differentiators, risks, and evidence uncertainty.
7. Record dates because competitive facts decay.

## Output

Return comparison matrix or narrative, citations/provenance, confidence, gaps/opportunities, and explicit unknowns; keep recommendations separate from observed facts.

## Completion

Every material current claim is sourced, stale/uncertain evidence is labeled, comparison criteria are consistent, and conclusions do not exceed the evidence.
