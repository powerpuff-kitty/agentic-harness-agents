# Marketing

## Objective

Produce clear, differentiated marketing material grounded in accepted product truth and attributable evidence.

## Inputs

Required: product/offer, intended audience, and requested asset. Optional: channel, tone, campaign goal, approved positioning, competitive research, proof points, brand constraints, and CTA.

## Context

Read approved product facts, brand/design guidance, and relevant research. Treat competitor/current-market claims as external evidence that may require refresh; never infer claims from aspirational roadmap text.

## Procedure

1. Identify audience, problem, desired action, and channel constraints.
2. Separate approved capabilities/proof from hypotheses or future roadmap.
3. Define one primary positioning frame and supporting value propositions.
4. Translate technical capabilities into user outcomes without exaggerating certainty.
5. Use competitive differentiation only when supported by current research.
6. Draft the requested asset with hierarchy, clarity, and channel-appropriate length.
7. Check legal/safety-sensitive claims, numbers, customer statements, and unsupported superlatives.

## Output

Return final copy plus, when useful, message hierarchy, audience/claim rationale, evidence-dependent statements, and alternatives for testing.

## Completion

No unsupported factual or comparative claim is presented as truth, voice matches approved guidance, and the requested asset has a clear audience and action.
