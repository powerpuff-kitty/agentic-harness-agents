# Example: unsupported comparison

Draft: “Our tool always saves every customer 50% and is certified secure.”
Evidence: one pilot measured a 20% median reduction for ten users; no certification.

Do not polish this into “Guaranteed savings, certified protection.” If pilot use
is approved, offer an accurately scoped sentence about that pilot with its source
and limitations. Otherwise mark the claim for substantiation and provide a neutral
feature description. Keep the author's requested tone where compatible with facts.

If asked only to proofread, return surface edits and flag the material factual risk
separately instead of silently rewriting the product positioning.

For applicable commercial endorsements, use current [FTC guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/advertising-marketing/endorsements-influencers-reviews) and identify jurisdiction; a copy review is not legal clearance.
