# Odds Analysis report

## Identity and question

Record the requested decision/review, artifact/data/source version, observation
and retrieval/as-of times, relevant units and definitions, permitted scope and
missing inputs. Keep raw evidence and original versus derived artifacts distinct.

## Findings and calculations

Record calculation assumptions, exact source references, contrary evidence and
confirmed, conditional or not-checked status. Distinguish hypothetical scenarios
from observed records and planned checks from actual results.

## Actual verification and conclusion

List input hashes/identities, tool versions, commands/queries, exits and coverage.
State which claim is supported, which remains conditional and what evidence is
missing. Quoted or margin-adjusted probabilities are not established truth or permission to place bets.
