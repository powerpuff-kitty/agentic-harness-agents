# Market Opportunity Research

## Objective and inputs

Assess a specific opportunity and the evidence needed for a business decision.
Establish product/problem, geography, buyer versus user, segment, time horizon,
revenue/unit definition, existing research and authorized sources. Use the
[method](references/method.md) for sizing, overlap and demand evidence.

## Procedure

1. Define the decision and falsifiable problem/demand hypotheses; distinguish a
   category trend, reachable buyers, actual use and willingness to pay. Identify
   substitutes including manual work, doing nothing and incumbent workflows.
2. Gather current primary evidence where available: population/business counts,
   source definitions/dates, actual customer research/behavior and price/access terms.
   Preserve provenance, uncertainty and contradictory evidence. Synthetic inputs
   support an illustrative calculation only, not a real market claim.
3. Segment by mutually explicit scope and separate buyer, user, account/location
   and transaction units. Identify overlaps and excluded populations before adding
   counts. Top-down reports and competitor list prices are contextual, not validated
   reachable demand or proven willingness to pay.
4. Build transparent bottom-up ranges using compatible units, periods/currencies,
   geography, reachable counts, adoption/pricing assumptions and costs where relevant.
   Distinguish total/addressable/serviceable/reachable scenarios and a plausible
   initial segment; do not relabel an arbitrary fraction as obtainable market.
5. Reproduce arithmetic and sensitivity, showing source-backed inputs versus
   assumptions and how missing/overlapping data bounds the answer. Use current
   sources for unstable facts; do not invent live quotes, customer commitments,
   market-size estimates or precision unsupported by the inputs.
6. Triangulate with authorized research/behavior and competition. Recommend a
   next validation action and what result would change the decision; no outreach,
   purchase, ad spend or live experiment is automatically authorized.
7. Deliver the [opportunity report](assets/report-template.md), evidence/assumption
   ledger, scoped calculations/ranges, risks, negative evidence and next decisions.
   Use existing research/product/GTM owners; no new data provider is required.

## Completion

Definitions, units, source dates, overlap and sensitivity are explicit; demand,
pricing, market reach and financial projections remain distinct claims. Unavailable
customer evidence stays unknown and illustrative estimates stay labeled as such.
