# Example: pilot study to social and newsletter

Source: an approved report says 8 of 12 pilot participants completed onboarding
within one day in September; no comparison group or population inference.

Social hook: “In our September pilot, 8 of 12 participants finished onboarding
within a day.” Retain “pilot” and the denominator; link the report. A newsletter
can explain the onboarding steps and limitations. Do not say “Two-thirds of all
customers onboard instantly” or claim the feature caused faster onboarding.

An accessible chart description should carry the same population and timing.
If a requested channel has a supplied 200-character cap, count the actual draft;
link handling and platform display rules may require a separate platform check.
