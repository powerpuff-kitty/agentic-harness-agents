# Settlement contract review

## Rule identity and evidence

Capture venue/market/contract/outcome IDs, text/source version or hash, capture
and review-as-of time. Identify market-specific terms and applicable general rules
and their actual precedence. A title can omit the condition that decides payout;
do not substitute it for the contract or invent a precedence rule when sources
conflict. Retain conflicting versions and request the decisive missing evidence.

Normalize event, threshold, unit, inclusive/exclusive boundary, timezone/cutoff,
measurement/source, publication/revision policy, outcome set and payment unit.
Check postponements, cancellation/void/refund, dead heat/partial payout, fees,
collateral, proposal/dispute windows and finality where relevant. Rules and source
availability can change; pin the actual snapshot for the requested analysis.

## Synthetic comparison

Market A asks whether Team X wins after 90 minutes plus stoppage; Market B includes
extra time. Team X draws at 90 minutes and wins in extra time. Under those declared
terms, the events differ: A is not a regulation-time win and B can be a win.
This is not a price-arbitrage pair simply because titles say Team X wins. Actual
payout still depends on the full outcome/void/resolution contract.

A threshold of strictly above 100 differs from at least 100 when the measured value
is exactly 100. A deadline without timezone or a source without revision policy
can leave the outcome unresolved; do not choose the economically favorable meaning.

## Resolution versus event truth

[Polymarket resolution guidance](https://help.polymarket.com/en/articles/13364518-how-are-prediction-markets-resolved)
explains rule-based outcomes and resolution/dispute stages. It is not the full
contract for every market or proof of a current outcome. A proposal, a closed
trading UI and final settlement are different states. Do not post a bond or submit
a resolution to learn the answer. Verify the actual market/rule/status only with
approved evidence or permitted read-only access.

[Betfair general rules](https://support.betfair.com/app/answers/detail/exchange-general-rules)
include rule interactions and dead-heat treatment. Venue/sport/market-specific
terms still matter; do not apply one general example to every bookmaker or event.
Both references reviewed 2026-10-14; no current fee, deadline, jurisdictional
eligibility or legal conclusion is supplied by this skill.

## Report decisions

Confirmed: two captured contracts have materially different event definitions.
Conditional: supplied outcome facts satisfy one interpretation, but timezone or
rule version is missing. Not checked: no authoritative resolution/status evidence
is available. In each case, distinguish hypothetical payout reasoning from an
actual claim, withdrawal, legal certification or transaction.
